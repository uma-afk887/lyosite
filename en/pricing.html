Pricing

Freeze-drying cost & pricing

Honest ranges and the factors that actually drive the price. Freeze-drying is billed on machine time, not on weight alone — so the water you start with and the cycle it needs matter more than the kilos you end up with.

What drives the cost

  • Water content & cycle time — the wetter the product and the more delicate the cycle, the longer the machine runs.
  • Volume & frequency — recurring cycles and full loads lower the cost per kilo; a capacity subscription lowers it further.
  • Process — vacuum microwave drying is often cheaper than freeze-drying when the product doesn't need to rehydrate.
  • Packaging — barrier film, nitrogen flushing and oxygen absorbers add cost but buy shelf life.
